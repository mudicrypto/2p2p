# Delete your account

_Last updated 2026-11-18. DRAFT for review by counsel: complete every [BRACKETED] item for each country before launch. Shown in the app at /{locale}/legal/delete-account._

## How to ask

In the app or on the website, open Account and choose "Delete account", which brings you here. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm we received it within 2 working days and may ask you to verify that the request is yours.

## Before we delete

Finish or cancel your open orders and close your ads. Withdraw any crypto left in your wallet: we cannot delete an account that still holds funds or has an open order or dispute.

## What we delete

Within 30 days we close your account and delete your profile, payment details, chat messages, attachments, push tokens and preferences, and you can no longer sign in.

## What we must keep

Anti-money-laundering law requires us to keep identity verification results and transaction records for [5–10] years after the account is closed. We keep only these records, protected and access-controlled, and delete them when the legal period ends.
