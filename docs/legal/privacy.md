## 1. Controller

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS]. Data protection officer: [DPO EMAIL].

## 2. Data we process

Account data: email, phone, Telegram chat id (if you sign in with or link Telegram), country, language, nickname, settings (business hours, break mode, anti-phishing code, saved searches, price alerts). Verification data from our KYC provider: name, date of birth, document and liveness results, proof of address. Device and log data: device fingerprint, IP address, user agent, last time you were active. Trading data: orders, chat, uploaded receipts and evidence and the results of automatic checks on them, disputes, feedback, follows and blocks, invite relationships and rewards. Payment details you add (stored encrypted). Wallet data: addresses, transactions.

## 3. Why we process it

To provide the service (contract); to verify identity, screen sanctions, prevent fraud and meet anti-money-laundering, tax and record-keeping duties (legal obligation); to secure the Platform, detect scams and improve the service (legitimate interests); marketing only with consent.

## 4. What other users see

Your nickname, verification level, trust score and trading statistics, whether you are online or when you were last active, break mode and business hours. In a trade, your counterparty sees what is needed to complete it: the payment details you chose to use, chat messages and files you send, and, for sellers, plain facts about the buyer that call for extra care (for example a new account) and the buyer's verified name where available.

## 5. Automated checks and profiling

We compute a trust score from your trading history, check uploaded receipts and chat messages for known scam patterns, and score orders for fraud risk. These checks can show cautions or require manual review; they do not by themselves decide a dispute. You can ask for a human review of any automated decision that affects you.

## 6. Sharing

With our processors under data processing agreements: identity verification, custody, blockchain analytics, payment verification, messaging (email, SMS, push notifications, Telegram), hosting, and, where enabled, an AI service provider [AI PROVIDER] that translates chat messages you ask to translate and reads payment receipts for the automatic checks; it may not use your data to train its models [CONFIRM IN CONTRACT]. With your counterparty as described above. With authorities where legally required.

## 7. Retention and security

Verification and transaction records are kept for [5–10] years after account closure as required by AML law; other data for as long as needed for the purpose. Personal data and payment details are encrypted at field level; access is role-based and audited.

## 8. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure (subject to legal retention), restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your supervisory authority. Contact [DPO EMAIL].

## 9. International transfers
