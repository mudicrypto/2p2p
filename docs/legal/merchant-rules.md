# Merchant Rules

_Last updated 2026-11-20. DRAFT for review by counsel: complete every [BRACKETED] item for each country before launch. Shown in the app at /{locale}/legal/merchant-rules._

## Who these rules are for

Everyone who posts ads on parley ("merchants"). Verified merchants additionally hold a security deposit and follow the merchant program terms shown in the Merchant centre.

## Ads

Ads must show a real price you will honour, real limits and real payment methods in your own name. Do not post duplicate ads to crowd the market. Terms and automatic messages must not ask users to contact you outside parley, pay to other accounts, or pay any extra fee.

## Serving orders

Respond within the payment window, release promptly once the money is in your account, and use break mode or business hours when you are unavailable. Orders cancelled or disputed because of you lower your completion rate and trust score.

## Promotion

Promotion places an ad in labelled Promoted spots; it never changes the price ranking of the main list and is shown only to users whose filters match. Promotion fees are not refundable once started. We may remove promotion from ads that break these rules, without refund.

## API access

API keys are personal, must be kept secret and are rate-limited. Automated pricing must keep ads accurate; repeated stale or misleading prices can lead to suspension of API access.

## Enforcement

Breaking these rules can lead to warnings, removal of ads or promotion, loss of merchant level or badge, forfeiture of rewards, and account suspension. Serious cases are reported to authorities.
