# Risk Disclosure

_Last updated 2026-11-20. DRAFT for review by counsel: complete every [BRACKETED] item for each country before launch. Shown in the app at /{locale}/legal/risk._

## Crypto-assets are high risk

Prices can fall sharply and you can lose all the money you exchange. Stablecoins can lose their peg. Crypto-assets are generally not covered by deposit guarantee or investor compensation schemes.

## Peer-to-peer risks

You deal with other users. Fiat payments can be reversed, frozen by banks, or come from third parties; reversible payment methods carry chargeback risk. Escrow protects the crypto side only if you follow the trading rules. The insurance fund may compensate approved bank-freeze claims within its limits but is not a guarantee.

## Automatic checks are not a guarantee

Receipt checks, scam cautions, trust scores and translations help you decide but can be wrong or miss a fake. Always confirm the money is in your account before you release crypto.

## Operational risks

Blockchain networks, custodians and payment providers can fail or be delayed. Sending crypto to a wrong address or network is usually irreversible.

## Regulatory and tax

Rules differ by country and can change; services may be restricted in your country. You are responsible for your taxes.
