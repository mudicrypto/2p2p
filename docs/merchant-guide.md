## Why trade on parley

- Every trade is protected by escrow: the seller's crypto is locked before the buyer pays, so you never send crypto first and never pay without the crypto being held.
- Takers pay no fee. Makers pay a small fee only on completed trades (0.1% by default, lower at higher merchant levels). [LAUNCH OFFER, e.g. "0% maker fee for launch merchants until DATE".]
- You get tools pro traders expect: business hours, break mode, auto-reply, buyer conditions, bulk actions, analytics, promoted ads, a shop link and an API.

## 1. Set up your account (10 minutes)

- Sign up, verify your email and turn on two-factor authentication (Account → Security). You need 2FA to release crypto.
- Choose a short, memorable nickname (Account → Trading profile). It becomes your shop link: [DOMAIN]/en/m/YourNickname.
- Add the payment methods you will receive money on (Wallet → Payment methods). Only add accounts in your own name.
- Set an anti-phishing code: every real email from parley will show it, so you can spot fake ones.
- Optional but recommended: complete ID verification and apply to the merchant program (Merchant centre) for the verified badge and lower fees.

## 2. Post your first ad

- Go to My ads → Post ad. Choose buy or sell, the asset and currency, and a fixed price or a floating price (a % above or below the market rate that follows the market automatically).
- Set the amount you want to trade and the minimum and maximum per order. Smaller minimums bring more first-time buyers.
- Pick a payment window (15 minutes is standard) and write short, clear terms. Do not ask anyone to contact you outside parley; such terms are blocked.
- Who can trade with you: optionally require a verification level, a minimum account age or a minimum number of completed trades.
- Auto-reply: a first message sent automatically when an order opens, e.g. "Hi! Pay exactly the amount shown, include the order reference, then tap I have paid."

## 3. Be there when buyers come

- Business hours (My ads) show your ads only during the hours and days you choose. Break mode hides all your ads instantly, e.g. at lunch.
- Turn on notifications: install the app for push alerts, and connect Telegram (Account → Telegram alerts) to get "buyer paid" messages anywhere.
- Fast release builds your reputation: your usual release time is shown next to every ad.

## 4. Handle an order safely

- When the buyer marks the order paid, open your banking app and check that the exact amount has arrived. A screenshot is not money.
- Check the warnings parley shows you: facts about the buyer (new account, first trade, large amount for little history), automatic receipt checks (reused file, edited image, old image) and cautions on scam phrases in chat.
- Never release because of "payment pending", "my bank is slow, release first", "refund to my other account" or "pay a fee to unlock". These are scams.
- If the money arrived correctly, enter your 2FA code and tap Money received. If it did not arrive, do not release: wait for the timer and open an appeal. The crypto stays locked until an arbiter decides.
- Prefer payment methods that cannot be reversed. Card and PayPal-type payments can be charged back weeks later.

## 5. Disputes

- Open an appeal from the order after the timer ends. Explain what happened and attach evidence (bank statement screenshot, transaction id).
- Both sides see the progress: opened → evidence → arbiter reviewing → decision. Most disputes are decided within [SLA].
- If a bank freezes your account because of a payment you received on parley, submit a freeze report from the completed order with the bank's letter. Approved claims can be paid from the insurance fund within its limits.

## 6. Grow

- Your trust score (0–100) grows with completed trades, completion rate, good feedback, account age, verification and few disputes. Higher scores win more buyers.
- Share your shop link on social media: buyers see only your offers and trade in one tap.
- Promote an ad (My ads → Promote) to appear in the labelled Promoted spots above the offer list for a small daily fee in USDT.
- Use My ads → Your last 30 days to see orders, completion and volume per ad, and bulk actions to pause, resume or reprice many ads at once.
- Invite other traders with your invite code (Invite friends) and earn the invite reward when they complete their first trade.
- Automate with the merchant API: create keys in the Merchant centre and update prices from your own systems ([DOMAIN]/docs: merchant-api).
