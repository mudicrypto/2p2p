## Store texts

**App name** (30 max): `parley: P2P crypto, escrowed`

**Short description** (80 max):
> Buy and sell crypto with people. Every trade protected by escrow.

**Full description** (4,000 max):

> parley lets you buy and sell crypto directly with other people, paying with the bank and payment methods you already use.
>
> **Protected by escrow**
> When you start a trade, the seller's crypto is locked in escrow before you send any money. It is released to you only when the seller confirms your payment has arrived. If something goes wrong, open a dispute and the funds stay frozen until an arbiter decides.
>
> **See exactly what you get**
> Type how much you want to pay and parley shows how much crypto you will receive from each offer, best first. No hidden spreads: the price you see is the price you trade at.
>
> **Know who you trade with**
> Every trader has a passport built from their real trade history: completed trades, completion rate, how fast they usually release, disputes and feedback.
>
> **Built for safety**
> • Two-factor authentication and biometric confirmation before releasing crypto
> • Payment details shown only to your trade counterparty
> • In-app chat that blocks attempts to take the deal off the platform
> • Upload payment receipts and evidence right in the trade
>
> **Your wallet**
> Deposit, withdraw and transfer USDT, BTC and ETH. See what is available and what is locked in open trades at a glance.
>
> Available in 26 languages, with light and dark themes.
>
> parley is a peer-to-peer platform: fiat payments go directly between users. Availability, limits and verification requirements depend on your country.

## Data safety answers

Data is **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS): Yes. Users can **request deletion**: Yes, at `/en/legal/delete-account`. Shared with third parties: No. Identity verification, custody and hosting providers process data on parley's behalf, and showing a counterparty your payment details happens because you start a trade, so neither counts as sharing.

| Data type | Collected | Required? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | Required | Identity verification (KYC), payment matching |
| Email address | Yes | Required | Account, security alerts |
| Phone number | Yes | Optional | Account security |
| Address | Yes (KYC level 2+) | Required for higher limits | Identity verification |
| Other info: date of birth, ID document results | Yes | Required for trading | Legal obligation (AML) |
| Financial info: payment details you add | Yes | Required to sell | Shown to your counterparty in a trade |
| Financial info: purchase/transaction history | Yes | Required | Service, legal record keeping |
| Messages: in-app messages | Yes | Optional | Trade chat, dispute evidence |
| Photos | Yes | Optional | Payment receipts, dispute evidence |
| Device or other IDs | Yes | Required | Fraud prevention, push notifications |
| Location | No (country is self-declared) | n/a | n/a |
| Contacts, calendar, health, audio | No | n/a | n/a |

No data is used for advertising. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.
